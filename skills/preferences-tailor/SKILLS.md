# Preferences Tailor

## Instructions
1. Analyze the user's prompt for:
   - **Mood**: (e.g., romantic, family-friendly, quick bite).
   - **Budget**: (e.g., $, $$, $$$).
2. Check the current **Time of Day** to ensure the restaurant serves appropriate meals (e.g., breakfast vs. dinner).
3. If the user's needs are vague, ask clarifying questions about their mood or budget before finalizing a suggestion.
